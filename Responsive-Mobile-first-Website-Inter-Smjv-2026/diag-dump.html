    Every rule, one place

    General rules, the code of conduct, and the playing conditions for all ten sports. Last updated 20 November 2026.

    Eligibility

    1. Open to every bonafide resident of the eight SMJV hostel branches.
    2. Carry your hostel ID and the digital player card at every venue.
    3. A player may represent one hostel only, in any number of sports.

    Registration

    1. Team entries close at 6 PM on 30 November 2026.
    2. Individual sports accept on-the-spot entries until Day 1, 9 AM, subject to draw size.
    3. Changes to a team sheet are allowed until 24 hours before that team's first match.

    Kit and conduct on the field

    1. Studs are allowed on the Main Ground; non-marking shoes only in the Indoor Hall.
    2. Jewellery must be taped or removed before play.
    3. Team captains report to the duty desk 15 minutes before the scheduled start.

    Walkovers and delays

    1. A team not on the field within 10 minutes of the call loses the match.
    2. Weather delays are decided by the sports secretary; the schedule page is the only official notice.

    Frequently asked

    Who can take part?

    Any resident student of the eight SMJV hostel branches in Vadodara. Alumni and day scholars can join the volunteers team but cannot play.

    How do I register a team?

    Register individually first and pick your sports. Your hostel sports secretary then submits the team sheet before 6 PM on 30 November.

    Can I play more than one sport?

    Yes. Check the timetable before selecting ΓÇö the schedule flags clashes across your chosen sports on the registration success screen.

    Where do I collect my kit?

    The kit desk is at the hostel reception, open 8 AM to 8 PM from 12 December. Bring your player ID card.

    What if it rains?

    Outdoor matches are paused and rescheduled the same evening. The schedule page and notifications carry the official update ΓÇö nothing else does.

    Are meals included?

    Yes, all registered players get breakfast, lunch and dinner at the mess hall for all five days. Carry your ID card to the counter.

    Is there a dress code for the cultural night?

    Traditional or smart casual. Dandiya sticks are provided at the ground; please do not bring your own.

    Who do I contact in an emergency?

    Call the 24-hour helpline on +91 98250 40120. The medical room is beside the Main Ground pavilion.
