    Layout and motion

    Spacing

    4/8 scale: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96. Sections breathe at 64px on phones, 96px on desktop. Breakpoints at 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 — layout reflows, nothing shrinks as a whole page.

    Motion

    160ms for state feedback, 240ms for view and dialog transitions, 360ms for scroll reveals. Only transform and opacity animate. The marquee pauses on hover and stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.
